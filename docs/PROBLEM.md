# Problem, audience and success criteria

**Problem.** Can a small, approved local corpus answer control questions with traceable citations and abstain when the corpus does not support an answer?

**Audience.** Researcher or learner who needs source-grounded retrieval rather than fluent unsupported generation.

**Success criterion.** Lexical and concept-expanded retrieval are benchmarked on a golden set; every returned answer includes source IDs; unsupported questions trigger abstention.

Out of scope: production deployment, client outcomes, regulatory approval and any claim not present in the evidence manifest.
