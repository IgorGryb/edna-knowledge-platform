# EDNA — management case study

Private review draft. Design study, not a client deployment.

## Problem

Research outputs become unreliable when claims lose their source, version, access restriction or correction history.

## Approach

An operating model for governed research and knowledge reuse.

The design connects source evidence, explicit assumptions, operating ownership and model checks. Its primary output is a decision package that can be handed to an execution team.

## Key decision

Use a small versioned corpus, lexical baseline and citation review before adding semantic retrieval breadth.

## Operating design

1. Approve and classify source subset
2. Capture stable IDs and extraction provenance
3. Retrieve candidate passages
4. Review answer and citation entailment
5. Release internal note and monitor source changes

## Economics and validation

Cost per accepted research note = analyst/reviewer time + extraction/retrieval cost. Compare manual search and assisted search under the same question set. Time saved is a hypothesis, not earned revenue.

Testable hypothesis: Provenance and abstention improve reliability at acceptable review cost.

Negative check: Replace a source version and ask an unanswerable question; invalidate dependent notes and abstain.

## Outcome and limits

The current result is a documented management model with explicit handoffs, metrics and validation cases. Historical technical prototypes are supporting evidence only. Market adoption, customer impact and production operation are not established.

A high hit rate on a tiny golden set does not establish answer correctness or generalization.

## Next decision

Expand evaluation to citation support and contradiction handling before proposing service scale.
